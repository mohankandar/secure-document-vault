4 · Viewer

Document viewer

Same three columns as today, with clearer actions and room for the AI layer.

  • Actions have words: Find, Download, Print, Redact. Actions the role may not use are hidden, not greyed out (change 6). Redact is shown in red because it is permanent.
  • Left column folds away with the button at its top. Page previews look like the pages (change 7).
  • Title is the document and the borrower, not just a file name.
  • Ask tab (later release): every answer shows its working and its source; clicking a source jumps to the highlighted text on the page.
  • Key terms (later release): pulled from the document, each linked to its page.
  • The AI only reads the redacted version, so it can never reveal what is under a black box.
