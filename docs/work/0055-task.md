# 0055: Restrictions page

Written: 2026-11-23, by the design chat. Design proposed by the design chat and **approved by the owner on 2026-11-23**. Branch `feature/restrictions-page` from the newest `release/vero-r7`. Vero-only.

**The owner approves:** code and screens; no schema change expected (if one is needed for speed, it is approved). No new dependency.

A **read-only** Business Admin overview of everything restricted, in the R7 look (closest model: the Legal holds and Approvals pages), each section linking to the page that acts:
1. **Confidential and Highly Confidential documents:** counts by sending system.
2. **Records with a contract-level restriction:** count, and a paged list of their primary identifiers with level and date set (no titles, no other fields).
3. **Recent level changes:** who, when, scope (document or contract), from, to; the last 30 days, paged. Reasons stay out (as in the audit).
4. **Lowering requests waiting:** count and oldest, linking to Approvals.
5. **Legal holds:** count of holds and documents held, linking to Legal holds.
6. **Open quarantine incidents:** count, documents, and "being prepared" ones (counts only for the Business Admin: no names of documents), linking nowhere a Business Admin can't go.
7. Admin views show the record's primary identifier only, never the internal document ID, titles, file names or content (separation rule 4).
8. Must answer quickly at a million documents.

Tests for each section, the role check (only Business Admin), and that nothing restricted leaks (no titles, file names, content, reasons). `npm test`, full build, both schema checks, `ui:capture` with a new step and screenshot.
