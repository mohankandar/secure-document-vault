# 0071: Security fixes 4 (Vero-only Low findings)

Written: 2026-11-24 by the SDV design chat (LV-19: decided as recommended). Branch `fix/security-4` from `release/vero-r7`. Findings: `docs/work/security-findings.md` (published). The crypto-related ones (VSEC-12, 13, 14, 21) wait for the integration (platform-crypto, erase log).

**Approved:** code, screens, config, a Liquibase changeset if needed, new codes.

1. **VSEC-15 print:** Highly Confidential pages drawn for print or high zoom carry a light watermark with the person's name and the time; print-quality images are issued only against a used grant (normal viewing keeps screen quality). Say in the guides that screenshots can't be stopped.
2. **VSEC-17 quarantine release:** releasing documents one by one from an incident is limited to 25 per person per incident per day; above that, the whole-incident release (Business Admin approval) is the way. The incident's creator sees every single release in the incident's history.
3. **VSEC-32 legal holds:** releasing a hold needs a **second Business Admin** (not the one who placed it), as a request in Approvals. One Business Admin alone can't place and lift a hold.
4. **VSEC-33:** incident names and reasons are shown only to Stewards who can open at least one document in the incident.
5. **VSEC-28:** `GET /api/v1/system/info` needs sign-in and no longer names profiles or schema state to anyone but IT Admin and Platform Ops.
6. **VSEC-35:** a per-sending-system intake rate limit (setting, generous default), answered with the busy answer (SDV-SYS-018 with `Retry-After`).
7. **VSEC-26:** document in the IT guide how long a removed grant or group keeps working (30 s cache; the token's life, at most 2 hours).
8. Update `security-findings.md`: each of these marked fixed with its PR.
