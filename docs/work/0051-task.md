## 1. No limit on quarantine (owner decision)

Whatever matches the filter is quarantined: no cap, even a million documents. Replace the 1000-document limit (0048 decision 4) with:
- **Everything matching is blocked at once.** When the Steward confirms, the incident stores its filter and a cut-off (the confirm time and the highest document already received). From that moment every document matching the filter **and received before the cut-off** is "Temporarily unavailable" to everyone but reviewers, on every path (search, record panel, viewer, print, download, tags, comments, level changes, retention), even before its own quarantine row exists. Documents arriving after the cut-off are not caught (holding new arrivals stays V2).
- **The per-document rows are written in the background, in batches** (for the review list, the release, the exposure report and retention). The job survives a restart and continues where it stopped; running a batch twice changes nothing.
- **Progress** on the incident: "412,000 of 1,000,000 done", and the incident shows "being prepared" until done. Review and release work on the documents already written; whole-incident release waits until the job is done.
- **The count is shown before confirming**, as today.
- **Release of a whole incident** also ends the filter blocking. A single release frees that one document even though the filter still matches it.
- Tests: blocking is immediate for a large match (e.g. 20,000 documents in the PostgreSQL test) on every path; a document received after the cut-off is not blocked; the job resumes after a simulated restart without duplicates; single and whole-incident release; retention skips filter-blocked documents; the exposure report is complete once the job is done.
