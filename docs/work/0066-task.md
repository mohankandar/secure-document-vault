# 0066: Redaction of a document on legal hold (VSEC-2, owner decision)

Written: 2026-11-24 by the SDV design chat. **Owner decision (2026-11-23):** a document on legal hold may be permanently redacted **only with the Business Admin's approval**, and the original is **kept sealed** until the hold is released, then destroyed. Branch `fix/held-redaction` from `release/vero-r7`. Vero-only.

**Approved:** code, screens, a Liquibase changeset, new codes, a new approval request type.

1. A Steward's corrective redaction on a held document becomes a **request** (reason kept in the request, not the audit), decided by a Business Admin in Approvals (one at a time, no self-approval, expiry like other requests). Check the hold under the same document row lock as staging.
2. On approval the redacted version becomes the one people see; the **original is kept sealed**: only a Restricted Steward can open it, it is never printed or downloaded except through the existing Highly Confidential grant, and admins never see its content.
3. When the hold is released, the sealed original is destroyed (audited; the erase-log hook comes with platform-core). If several holds apply, after the last one.
4. Documents not on hold: unchanged.
5. Update `docs/api/error-codes.md` (the line that said redaction of held documents is intended) and the admin guide text.

Tests for each; full build, both schema checks, `npm test`, `ui:capture` with a step. Report `docs/work/0066-report.md` with "No other product's name or behaviour in code". Merge on green `ci-passed`.
