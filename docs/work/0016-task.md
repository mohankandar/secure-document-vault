## Part A: setup status (backend)

`GET /api/v1/setup/status` for the three roles above. Every step is worked out from real state, never ticked by hand:

| Step | Done when | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Sign-in works | the setup command's check passed and someone has signed in since | Installer |
| 2 Key service is running | a live check now | Installer |
| 3 Storage added | an in-use volume exists and can be written | Tenant IT |
| 4 Add a sending system | at least one sending system exists | Tenant IT |
| 5 How long to keep documents | a rule from the worksheet or the Business admin exists ("default" shows as open but doesn't block) | Worksheet |
| 6 Search fields and record title | set for at least one sending system, from the worksheet or by the Business admin (defaults: open, not blocking) | Worksheet |
| 7 Give people their roles | someone with each role has signed in at least once | Tenant IT |
| 8 First delivery arrives | at least one delivery received | Automatic |

- For step 7, use the audit if it already records sign-ins with roles. Otherwise add a small record of "first sign-in per role" (one migration). Say which in the report.
- Setup is **complete** when steps 1–4, 7 and 8 are done. Then the status reports complete and keeps the date.

## Part B: Tenant IT Setup tab

1. While setup isn't complete, Tenant IT has a **Setup** tab first and lands on it.
2. Header:
   - "Set up Vero";
   - "Eight steps before people can use Vero. You can leave and come back; progress is saved.";
   - "5 of 8 done";
   - "The business decisions came from the setup worksheet."
3. Each step shows:
   - its number or a tick, the title, and why it matters in one line;
   - who does it (BY Installer / Tenant IT / Worksheet / Automatic);
   - its state ("Done · 17 Nov", "Next step", "Not started", "Waiting for step 4");
   - its action ("Add sending system" opens the editor from task 0010; "Show the groups" lists the exact group names to create in the sign-in system).
4. When setup is complete, the tab disappears. The list stays under **About › Setup history**, with dates and who did each step.

## Part C: Business admin review banner

1. On every Business admin page, until someone chooses **Looks right**, show:
   - "Set for you during installation";
   - "Your IT team gave us these answers from the setup worksheet. Please check them; you can change either any time.";
   - the two decisions, each with **Review** (opens Keeping documents or Search fields) and **Looks right**.
2. If the worksheet had no answer, list the decision as open with the safe default in use ("Keep forever until you decide").
3. "Looks right" is stored for the installation, not per browser, and audited with the person's name. Add the minimum needed to store it.

## Part D: Platform operations

1. On Overview, while setup isn't complete, show a tile:
   - "Setup: 5 of 8 done";
   - "Next: Tenant IT adds a sending system.";
   - "See the checklist →", which opens a read-only copy;
   - **Copy setup status**, which copies plain text for the install notes, with no personal data.
2. After setup is complete, the tile goes. About shows the setup history.
